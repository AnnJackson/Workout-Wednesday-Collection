# 2021 Week 32 | Tableau: Visualize our Survey Data

- **Published:** August 11, 2021
- **Original post:** https://www.workout-wednesday.com/2021w32tab/
- **Author:** Ann Jackson

## Introduction

The Workout Wednesday team recently wrapped up a survey for the community gauged at understanding what the community is interested in and what areas of opportunity the team may have.

During the latest team meeting, Ann was struck with the idea that this was the perfect opportunity to get a twofer — practice visualizing survey data AND have the community analyze and draw their own conclusions about WOW.

On that note: this week's challenge. You'll be using Tableau to visualize the responses of 63 participants. Ann intentionally pared down all the questions to focus on the number one skill the community mentioned interest in: Table Calculations.

For the sake of the challenge, you're not allowed to use LODs (even though that is very tempting). You must only use Table Calculations and normal calculated fields. You are allowed to add on any additional visualizations for questions or change up how you would visualize — have fun with the data!

## Requirements

- Dashboard Size: 1400px by 1800px
- # of Sheets – up to you
- Create donut charts that show % of participants for each tool, make them interactive filters
- Create a bar chart showing participation frequency that is also an interactive filter
- Build divergent bar charts paired with vertical bar charts to express how much respondents agree/disagree with 5 questions
- Build a grid (heatmap/highlight table) showing the rank and percentage of each of the categories by rank order (hint: aliased the Questions to have the rank title)
- Formatting:
  - Match all tooltips and dashboard dividers
  - Green-Orange-Teal for divergent bar chart combo
  - Purple continuous color palette is from Color Brewer set, if you don't have it, choose one you like

## Dataset

This week uses the WOW community survey data, lightly cleaned and reshaped, available via Google Drive (linked from the original post).
